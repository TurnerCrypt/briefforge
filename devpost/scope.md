# Hackathon Checklist — working title

Turn a pasted hackathon brief into a verifiable submission checklist.

## The Unique Kernel
Each requirement includes its source sentence, and unclear requirements are flagged for review. Users can check what the brief actually says rather than trusting an unsupported summary.

## Who It's For
Hackathon entrants reading long briefs and preparing their submissions. The learner selected this problem after screening hackathons.

## The Core Loop
Paste brief → review checklist → tick off requirements → export.

## Inspiration & Identity
No visual direction or final project name established yet. Decide these during product planning.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The learner chose this idea and agreed that missing buried requirements is the problem to address. Further personal motivation was not established.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A pasted brief produces checkable requirements with source sentences. Ambiguities remain visible. The user can review the checklist, mark items complete and export it.

## The POC Boundary
The approved first version covers the paste, review, check-off and export journey. Mandatory tools, repository links, video limits and deadline timezones are examples of requirements to surface when present.
